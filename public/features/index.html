Features

Everything Addit can do — automatically, on your device.

High-Quality Recording

Record phone calls and voice memos in crystal-clear quality. Background recording continues even when you switch apps.

  • Call recording with caller ID
  • Voice memo recording
  • Background recording support
  • Automatic file organization

AI-Powered Transcription

Convert speech to text automatically using industry-leading AI. Supports Deepgram and Gladia with 99+ languages.

  • Accurate speech-to-text
  • Multiple language support
  • Speaker identification
  • Searchable transcripts

Intelligent Extraction

AI analyzes transcriptions and automatically extracts actionable items like calendar events, reminders, and contact information.

  • Calendar event detection
  • Reminder extraction
  • Contact mention identification
  • Action item highlighting

Privacy First

All data stays on your device. No cloud storage, no servers. Use your own API keys for transcription and AI.

  • 100% local data storage
  • Your own API keys
  • No data sent to Addit servers
  • Hardware-encrypted key storage

Smart Search

Search across all your recordings and transcriptions using semantic AI-powered search or keyword lookup.

  • Full-text transcript search
  • Semantic AI search
  • Filter by date or contact
  • Instant results

Contact Integration

Recordings are automatically linked to your contacts so you always know who you spoke with and what was discussed.

  • Caller identification
  • Contact-based filtering
  • Auto-save new contacts from calls
  • Works with your existing contacts
